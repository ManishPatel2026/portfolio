About

Design that

I'm a London-based senior graphic designer with close to two decades of experience across retail, packaging, and print. My work has shaped the visual identity of some of the UK's most recognised brands — from department stores to outdoor specialists.

I work best where design has to perform: where a package needs to stand out in a crowded aisle, where a campaign has to travel across dozens of formats, or where a brand system needs to hold together at scale.
