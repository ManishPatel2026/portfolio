Overview

Bringing the spirit
of the outdoors to poster designs.

My role here was to design and manage the end-to-end creative and print process for Mountain Warehouse’s monthly global poster rollouts—ensuring on-time delivery, brand consistency, and high-quality execution.


The monthly promotional print brief would include the need for design concepts, automation, data cleaning, competitor shops, art direction and project management.

01

Print briefing

Team huddle to review the current month's promotional brief including key deadlines and delivery dates.

02

Design Team Deliverables

Divide the brief into equally weight jobs for dsigners to action ahead of weekly catch-ups with management

03

Bulk sign-off

Before working on format iterations, sign-off bulk of the designs with management

04

Quality assurance & upload

I review all artworks along side management to ensure there are no errors before we upload to print.
